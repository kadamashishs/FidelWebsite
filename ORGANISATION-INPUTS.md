# Organisation inputs for the next website phase

Please provide approved information for the items below before publishing the next marketing iteration.

## Positioning

- Preferred public brand name and the relationship between Fidel Projects and Fidel Engineering Works
- Primary target customers and decision-makers
- Priority industries and geographic markets
- Typical project size, scope, and delivery stage
- Three reasons customers choose Fidel instead of another engineering or fabrication partner

## Proof and trust

- Verified number of years in operation
- Number of projects completed
- Approved project values, timelines, and outcomes
- Client names and written permission to display logos
- Client testimonials with name, title, company, and approval
- Certifications, registrations, safety records, and quality systems
- Insurance, compliance, and professional memberships

## Projects and capability

For each approved case study:

- Project title and industry
- Client/location details that may be published
- Client challenge
- Fidel scope of responsibility
- Equipment, systems, or services delivered
- Project timeline
- Verified business or operational result
- Approved project images and captions

## People and contact

- Approved team photographs and biographies
- Official phone and WhatsApp numbers
- Preferred enquiry email addresses
- Expected response time for new enquiries
- Service areas and office addresses
- Emergency or site-support contact, if offered

## Digital marketing

- Official LinkedIn and other social profiles
- Approved logo files, brand colours, and fonts
- Social sharing image, if different from the current project image
- Analytics property ID and conversion events to track
- Preferred form provider or CRM destination
- Cookie, privacy, and data-retention requirements

Do not publish metrics, client names, certifications, testimonials, or photographs until the organisation has approved them for public use.
