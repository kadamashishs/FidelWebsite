Who We Are

Fidel Project combines Fidel Engineering Works and Fidel Projects and HR Services LLP to deliver bold, integrated solutions across process industries and beyond.

From concept planning and design to on-site execution, we own mechanical and fabrication projects for Dairy, Ethanol, Beverages, Sugar, Chemical, Oil & Gas and Pharma plants with uncompromising focus on safety, quality and schedules.

Beyond delivery, we bring deep consulting and a powerful workforce — project managers, site engineers, supervisors and safety officers — so every phase is executed with discipline and accountability.

As a unified partner, Fidel Project helps you expand capacity, optimize layouts and de-risk greenfield and brownfield investments, so your teams can keep production running while we manage execution on the ground.
